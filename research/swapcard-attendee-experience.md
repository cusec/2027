# Swapcard attendee experience reference

Checked 2026-09-22 against Swapcard's own product pages and help center. These are capabilities of Swapcard, not claims about CUSEC's current site.

| Area | Verified Swapcard behavior | Source |
|---|---|---|
| People discovery | Searchable participant directory with custom fields, filters, visibility rules, and AI recommendations. Networking can start before the event. | [Participants page](https://help.swapcard.com/en/articles/8158937-people-creating-and-managing-the-participants-page), [networking](https://www.swapcard.com/features/event-networking) |
| Connections and meetings | Attendees can request connections, send messages, and book meetings from people or exhibitor profiles; accepted meetings join their schedule. Organizers can limit messaging and meetings by attendee group or ticket. | [networking](https://www.swapcard.com/features/event-networking), [engagement](https://www.swapcard.com/features/attendee-engagement-software) |
| Digital QR exchange | An attendee can display a QR code on their phone; another attendee scans it to add them as a contact. Printed individual badges are optional. The organizer must enable Lead Retrieval for the relevant attendee group. | [QR code setup](https://help.swapcard.com/en/articles/8158987-generating-and-using-qr-codes-for-event-attendees) |
| Agenda and participation | Attendees can register for sessions and build a personal schedule. Session pages can expose registrants, live chat, polls, Q&A, feedback, resources, and sponsor links, depending on configuration. | [session setup](https://help.swapcard.com/en/articles/8159012-get-started-with-sessions), [session preferences](https://help.swapcard.com/en/articles/9196588-managing-your-session-preferences) |
| Updates | The mobile app sends automatic notifications for messages, new contacts, requests, and upcoming sessions; organizers can configure event push notifications. Push needs the mobile app and device permission. | [default notifications](https://help.swapcard.com/en/articles/8159053-default-push-notifications-on-swapcard-what-to-expect), [engagement](https://www.swapcard.com/features/attendee-engagement-software) |
| Sponsor and career discovery | Exhibitors can manage profiles, team members, links, documents, and item listings. Items explicitly include job offers, with categories and filters. Swapcard also sells sponsored in-app placements. | [items and job offers](https://help.swapcard.com/en/articles/8159033-creating-and-importing-items-to-showcase-your-offerings), [exhibitor add-ons](https://help.swapcard.com/en/articles/9019470-unlock-event-success-with-swapcard-add-ons) |
| Exhibitor lead capture | Exhibitors can scan attendee QR or NFC badges, record notes and qualifications, and work from a shared lead dashboard; some capture works offline. The organizer can set scans to share contact details with both people or only the scanner. | [lead capture setup](https://help.swapcard.com/en/articles/8158993-setting-up-lead-capture), [Exhibitor Lead Center](https://www.swapcard.com/features/exhibitor-center) |
| CRM handoff | Swapcard markets lead export and CRM sync. Its documented exhibitor Zapier workflow sends new leads to HubSpot or Salesforce. The separate native HubSpot Studio connector documented in September 2026 imports HubSpot contacts and companies **into** Swapcard; it should not be read as evidence of outbound lead sync. | [exhibitor Zapier guide](https://help-attendees.swapcard.com/en/articles/9310833-send-your-leads-to-hubspot-salesforce-and-other-crms-with-zapier), [HubSpot Studio connector](https://help.swapcard.com/en/articles/14816896-setting-up-a-hubspot-integration-in-your-event), [lead export](https://www.swapcard.com/features/event-monetization) |

## Useful pattern for CUSEC

The strongest small-platform pattern is a post-purchase home with immediate utility: ticket and next event information, a short profile prompt, a few relevant people or sessions, sponsor jobs, and one clear announcement or upgrade action. A digital contact card can support in-person exchange without printed badges. That inference adapts Swapcard's documented capabilities; it is not a Swapcard recommendation or an implementation plan.
